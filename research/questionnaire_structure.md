# Questionnaire Structure

This document outlines the structure of the consumer research instrument. Question wording below is representative of the actual instrument design approach; it has been generalized for public sharing.

## Section A — Screening & Demographics

| # | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Do you purchase skincare products at least occasionally? | Single choice (screener — non-qualifiers exit) |
| A2 | Age band | Single choice |
| A3 | Gender | Single choice |
| A4 | General skin type (oily / dry / combination / sensitive / normal) | Single choice |
| A5 | Primary skin concern | Single choice |

## Section B — Current Purchasing Behavior

| # | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Typical monthly budget for skincare | Single choice (banded) |
| B2 | Primary purchase channel (in-store / brand website / marketplace / other) | Single choice |
| B3 | How often do you try a new skincare brand? | Single choice |

## Section C — Purchase Drivers

| # | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Rank the following in order of importance when choosing a skincare product: *Efficacy, Price, Ingredient safety/transparency, Brand reputation, Sustainability* | Ranking |
| C2 | Which of the following active ingredients are you actively looking for? (select all that apply) | Multiple choice |
| C3 | Preferred product texture (gel / cream / lotion / balm / oil) | Single choice |

## Section D — Sustainability Attitudes

| # | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | How familiar are you with the term "sustainable skincare"? | Likert (1–5) |
| D2 | How much do you trust sustainability claims made by skincare brands in general? | Likert (1–5) |
| D3 | Have you ever stopped using a product because a claim felt exaggerated or misleading? | Yes/No + optional open text |
| D4 | Would you pay a premium for a product with credible sustainability credentials? | Single choice (No / Small premium / Moderate premium / Large premium) |

## Section E — Packaging & Format Preferences

| # | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Preferred packaging format (tube / pump / jar / other) | Single choice |
| E2 | Reasons for that preference | Multiple choice |

## Section F — Open-Ended

| # | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | What's your biggest frustration with skincare products you've tried before? | Open text |

---

**Design notes:**
- Section C1's ranking format (rather than independent Likert ratings) is the single most important design choice in this instrument — it's what allows the analysis to say sustainability is *relatively* less important than efficacy, rather than just "also rated as important" (which ranking-free Likert scales tend to produce for almost every attribute).
- Skip logic: respondents who fail the A1 screener do not see Sections B–F.
